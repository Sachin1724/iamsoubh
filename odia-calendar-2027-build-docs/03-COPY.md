# 03 · Page Copy

This copy is final unless it says **[confirm]** or **[fill]**. Nothing in brackets goes live. Every string here should live in the config or content files (see `06-CONFIG-AND-CONTENT.md`), not hard-coded in components.

**Copy rules:**
- Romanised Odia stays in *italics* (Inter italic).
- No exclamation marks or emoji in buttons and labels, and no em dashes.
- `→ ↓ ↗` in buttons and links are rendered as SVG icons, not typed characters.
- `₹` is wrapped in `<span class="rupee">`.
- The Odia title ବାର ମାସେ ତେର ପରବ is always the SVG artwork.
