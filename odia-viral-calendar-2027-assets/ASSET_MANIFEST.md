# Asset Manifest

These are starter visual assets generated for prototyping the Odia Viral Calendar 2027 landing page.

## Important
- These are concept assets, not final production artwork.
- Replace the generated box and festival-card concepts with approved client photography/artwork.
- The Odia title rendered in generated raster concepts may contain inaccurate glyph shapes. Use the client's supplied SVG title artwork for all production display use.
- Never use generated people or fake testimonial screenshots as real customer proof.
- Product box concept does not confirm final dimensions or contents. The wooden stand is not included in this concept.
- SVG icons are editable vector files.
