# QA and Acceptance Checklist

## Visual
- [ ] Matches approved red/yellow/green/rice-white design system.
- [ ] Uses client's actual title artwork and product/card assets.
- [ ] No OATSIDE/HIPPEAS logos, copied assets, or copied page copy.
- [ ] All product contents pictured are confirmed included.
- [ ] No fake testimonial or generated buyer image appears as a real customer.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow at 320, 375, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px widths.
- [ ] Sticky CTA does not obscure content, fields, or footer.
- [ ] Reduced-motion setting disables non-essential animation.

## Content
- [ ] 1,500+ / 22 states / USA claims verified against 2026 records.
- [ ] All testimonial screenshots and quotes have permission.
- [ ] Festival dates match approved print-ready artwork and two panjis.
- [ ] ₹299 price and ₹339 later price approved.
- [ ] ₹49 shipping outside Odisha approved.
- [ ] Dispatch date confirmed in writing.
- [ ] December 15 refund promise approved and matches refund policy.
- [ ] Damage replacement policy and 48-hour window approved.
- [ ] Wooden stand not promised unless production is confirmed.
- [ ] No bracketed placeholders remain in production copy.

## UX and accessibility
- [ ] Every CTA has a clear, working action.
- [ ] Order CTA reaches the same order section/sheet.
- [ ] Inputs have visible labels and inline validation.
- [ ] Keyboard focus is visible and logical.
- [ ] Tap targets are at least 44×44px.
- [ ] Image alt text is present where needed.
- [ ] Story viewer works with touch, keyboard, and reduced motion.
- [ ] FAQ accordion has accessible state labels.

## Payment
- [ ] Quantity constrained to 1–3.
- [ ] Price and shipping total are correct for quantity/address.
- [ ] Payment stock cap is enforced by provider/server, not a fake counter.
- [ ] Razorpay UPI flow tested in Instagram/WhatsApp in-app browsers on Android and iPhone.
- [ ] Card/netbanking methods tested where enabled.
- [ ] ₹1 payment test completed, refunded, and visible in the correct account.
- [ ] Payment success verified authoritatively.
- [ ] Cancel/failure/pending flows do not falsely claim success.
- [ ] Sold-out switch disables all payment entry points.
- [ ] Thank-you route and order reference work.

## Legal and operations
- [ ] Shipping, refunds/cancellation, terms, privacy, contact, and pricing pages live.
- [ ] Razorpay website review/domain settings checked.
- [ ] WhatsApp support number works and is monitored.
- [ ] Order confirmation copy ready.
- [ ] Courier account and pincode coverage checked.
- [ ] Daily order export/reconciliation workflow ready.
- [ ] Access to order data restricted.

## Performance and tracking
- [ ] Hero optimized; below-fold media lazy-loaded.
- [ ] Initial page weight near or under the 2 MB working target.
- [ ] No live Instagram embeds.
- [ ] Meta PageView, InitiateCheckout, Purchase tested.
- [ ] Purchase event fires only for confirmed payment and is deduplicated.
- [ ] UTMs are created for all launch channels.
- [ ] OG preview tested in Instagram/WhatsApp sharing.

## Acceptance
Do not launch until payment, inventory, key offer terms, real assets, policies, and support contact are confirmed. If a requirement is not ready, remove the unconfirmed claim rather than replacing it with an assumption.
